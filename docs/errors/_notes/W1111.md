A JSX expression the importer could not turn into anything was left in the page
exactly as written, rather than dropped — losing content silently is worse than
carrying it across.

Typically `{items.map(i => i.name)}`, `{children}`, or a template literal.

## Fix it

Rewrite it as a template expression over front matter, a snippet variable, or a
[fact](/guides/fact-modelling), or move the logic into a component.

This one does need a person to open the page, which is why it counts against the
page rather than the project.

Not raised for what the importer already handles: `{{ … }}`, `{% … %}`,
`{# … #}` and directive props are recognised as Liyasa's own; `{' '}` is JSX's
explicit space and becomes a space; and `{NAME}` paired with an
`export const NAME = "literal"` in the same page becomes front matter plus
`{{ NAME }}`.
