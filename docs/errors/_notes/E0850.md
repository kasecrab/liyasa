Something metered — sandbox minutes, render jobs, bandwidth, storage, model
tokens — reached this plan's quota for the billing period. On a plan whose
overage setting is `pause`, which is what Free is, the feature stops rather
than continuing onto an invoice nobody agreed to.

## Fix it

The quota resets at the start of the next billing period and the pause lifts
with it; nothing has to be done to resume. To resume now, move the
organization to a plan with a larger quota:

```
liyasa org plan --set pro
```

A paused resource is paused on its own. Sandbox minutes running out does not
stop render jobs, so a verification that stops while screenshots keep working
is the expected shape rather than a second fault.

The quota and the cost basis for every metered resource are on the plan page,
and current consumption is at `GET /_liyasa/api/v1/org/usage`.

Related: [E0852](E0852.md) for AI credits, which are metered separately and run
out on their own schedule.
