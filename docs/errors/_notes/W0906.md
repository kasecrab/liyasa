`ai.models.embeddings` names a different model, or a different vector width, from the one the active index was built with.

## Fix it

Nothing is broken and nothing is lost: the old index answers every query until the new one is complete, and the swap is a single transaction. A dimension change creates a new table rather than migrating rows, so the two never coexist in one shape.

What it costs is a full re-embedding of the corpus, which is why [W0911](/errors/W0911) may follow asking you to confirm it. To avoid the run, put the previous model back.

Chunk boundaries do not depend on the model, so a re-index re-embeds the corpus without re-chunking it.
